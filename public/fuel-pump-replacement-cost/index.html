Fuel Pump Replacement Cost

Fuel pump replacement costs $400–$900 including parts and labor. Most modern fuel pumps are electric and located inside the gas tank, making replacement more labor-intensive than older vehicles.

Average Cost

  • Economy fuel pump: $400–$600
  • OEM or premium pump: $550–$900
  • Labor: 2–4 hours depending on whether the tank needs to be dropped ($200–$400)

Symptoms of a Failing Fuel Pump

  • Engine sputters or hesitates at high speeds
  • Loss of power under load (climbing hills, towing)
  • Car won't start or is hard to start when hot
  • Whining noise from the fuel tank area
  • Check engine code P0087 (fuel pressure too low)

What Triggers P0087?

P0087 means fuel rail pressure is below the minimum required level. This is most commonly a failing fuel pump, but can also be caused by a clogged fuel filter (if your vehicle has an inline filter), a failed fuel pressure regulator, or a clogged fuel injector. A fuel pressure test is needed to properly diagnose.

Extend Pump Life

The fuel pump is cooled and lubricated by the fuel itself. Frequently running the tank below 1/4 tank stresses the pump by reducing cooling and increasing the risk of sucking sediment from the tank bottom. Keeping the tank above 1/4 tank is the single best way to extend fuel pump life.
