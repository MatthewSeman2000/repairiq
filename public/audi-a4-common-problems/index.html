Audi A4 Common Problems and Repair Costs

The Audi A4 is a benchmark luxury sport sedan — but out of warranty it can be expensive to maintain. Understanding the known issues by generation helps you buy smart and budget appropriately.

Timing Chain Tensioner Failure — 2.0T (B8: 2009–2016)

The 2.0T TFSI engine in B8-generation A4s (2009–2016) has a known timing chain tensioner failure, particularly on earlier examples. A plastic tensioner guide can break, causing catastrophic timing failure. Symptoms: rattling on cold start, rough running. Timing chain/tensioner replacement costs $1,500–$3,000. Always check for timing chain maintenance records on these years.

Oil Consumption — 2.0T TFSI (2009–2013)

The 2.0T in 2009–2013 A4s can consume 1 quart of oil per 1,000–2,000 miles. Audi extended warranty coverage and issued a fix (piston ring replacement) for affected vehicles. Verify fix was completed before buying. Check oil every fill-up on these years.

Carbon Buildup — All Direct Injection Engines

All direct-injection engines (2.0T, 3.0T) accumulate carbon deposits on intake valves since fuel doesn't wash them. Symptoms appear around 60,000–80,000 miles — rough idle, misfires, hesitation. Intake valve cleaning (walnut blasting) costs $400–$700. Budget for this as scheduled maintenance on any used Audi 2.0T.

DSG/S Tronic Transmission Service

The dual-clutch DSG transmission requires fluid changes every 40,000 miles — not 'lifetime' as Audi sometimes claims. Skipped DSG service leads to shudder and mechatronic failures. DSG service costs $300–$500. Mechatronic unit failure costs $1,500–$3,500.

Ownership Reality

Out-of-warranty Audi maintenance costs are significantly higher than Japanese brands. Budget $1,500–$3,000/year for maintenance on a used A4 over 60,000 miles. A pre-purchase inspection by an Audi specialist ($200–$300) is essential before buying any used A4 — especially B8 generation (2009–2016).
