Chevy Silverado Common Problems and Repair Costs

The Chevy Silverado is America's second best-selling truck and generally reliable — but the 5.3L V8 with Active Fuel Management (AFM) has a well-documented lifter failure problem that can cost $2,500–$4,000 to repair.

AFM/DFM Lifter Failure — 5.3L V8 (2014–2021)

The Active Fuel Management system deactivates cylinders to save fuel. The collapsing lifters used in this system are prone to failure, particularly on the 2014–2019 5.3L engines. Failure causes a loud ticking/tapping noise and often complete lifter collapse. Repair requires removing the heads and costs $2,500–$4,500. The fix many owners use is disabling AFM via a tune ($400–$800) and replacing lifters proactively. GM extended warranty coverage on some affected vehicles.

Transmission Shudder — 8L90/8L45 (2015+)

The 8-speed automatic in newer Silverados shudders during light throttle cruising around 25–45 mph. GM issued an updated fluid (Dexron HP) that resolves early shudder for many owners — a $150–$200 fluid change. If shudder persists, torque converter replacement runs $800–$1,500.

Paint and Rust Issues (2014–2019)

2014–2019 Silverados have paint adhesion issues — clear coat peeling, particularly on the roof and hood. GM extended the paint warranty on some vehicles. Repaint cost: $1,500–$3,500 per panel. Also inspect for rust on the lower rocker panels and behind the rear wheels on trucks from salt states.

Brake Wear (All Generations)

Silverado brakes wear faster than average due to truck weight. Front brake pads typically need replacement every 35,000–50,000 miles. Budget $300–$700 per axle for brake service. Rotors often need replacement at the same service on high-mileage trucks.

Best Years

  • Best: 2022+ (AFM replaced with improved DFM, transmission revised)
  • Good: 2019–2021 6.2L V8 (no AFM issues on this engine)
  • Watch: 2014–2021 5.3L — check for lifter noise and confirm AFM disable or warranty repair
  • Avoid: High-mileage 2007–2013 with original transmission fluid (6L80 fluid neglect causes failures)
